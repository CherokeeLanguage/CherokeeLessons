Cherokee Numbers Maze — ᏣᎳᎩ ᏗᏎᏍᏗ ᏚᎴᎾᎯᎯ — is a simple maze game that helps you
learn the Cherokee numbers. Wander around the maze and collect dice together
that match the challenge you were given — then explode them. Complete all of a
level's challenges and you move on to the next, larger level, with more numbers
to learn.

The app is free — the itch.io page's payment setting is "Name your own price",
so you can download it for free or leave a donation.

## Get Cherokee Numbers Maze

Every build is served by the app's itch.io page — play it right there in your
browser, or download builds for Android (APK that installs directly on your
device), Windows, macOS, and Linux.

### About

<details>
<summary>Gamepad Controls</summary>

* Left stick or DPAD: Move
* \[A\]: Fireball
* \[X\]: Pickup Blocks
* \[Y\]: Drop Blocks

</details>
<details>
<summary>Keyboard Controls</summary>

* Arrow Keys: Move
* \[F\]: Fireball
* \[D\]: Pickup Blocks
* \[S\]: Drop Blocks

You can also use \[SPACE\] or \[ENTER\] for Fireballs.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Installing</summary>

The Android APK installs directly on your device — Android may ask you to
allow installing apps from this source once. The Windows, macOS, and Linux
builds are plain downloads: grab them and run.

</details>

If you play it in your browser, you may have to click **in** the game window to
have audio.

Find an issue with the game? Please report it at the project's GitHub:
[NumbersMaze.godot/issues](https://github.com/CherokeeLanguage/NumbersMaze.godot/issues)
